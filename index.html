Candidate for 1Y Alumni Affairs Chair

Hi, I’m Tarari.

Before SOM, I worked as a grants manager at Aceli Africa, an agricultural finance nonprofit headquartered in Washington, D.C. Before that, I was a management consultant in London. These experiences taught me the importance of listening carefully, building strong relationships, and following through on commitments.

From my first visit as a prospective student through orientation, SOM students and alumni have consistently welcomed me with kindness and warmth. I’m running for 1Y Alumni Affairs Chair because I want to help foster the caring, welcoming community we have inherited from past generations of SOMers—and extend it to future ones.

As a former fundraiser, I bring experience stewarding external relationships and soliciting support. Most importantly, I want to learn what matters to my classmates and help turn those priorities into action.
