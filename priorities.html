My priorities

Building stronger connections, together.

Three starting points for a more useful, inclusive alumni experience. Details will be added as the campaign develops.

01

Listen & Act

I will listen first and act second. Our alumni engagement strategy should be shaped by the needs and preferences of our class. Your input will guide my priorities and actions.

02

Work & Life

Professional guidance is naturally a priority for us as MBA students, but our questions extend beyond work. I want to create space for candid conversations with alumni about balancing ambition with wellbeing, sustaining relationships, navigating major life transitions, and building a meaningful life after SOM.

03

Home & Abroad

SOM’s community extends far beyond the United States, and our alumni engagement should too. I want to connect classmates with alumni building careers around the world and make global pathways more visible.
